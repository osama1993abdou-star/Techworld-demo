Key Features of Power BI

Based on proven Power BI implementation experience, we help organizations unlock data value faster with practical, scalable analytics architecture.

1

Connection to Multiple Data Sources

Built-in connectors allow Power BI to connect with hundreds of data sources from Microsoft, Salesforce, and other platforms, whether on-premise or cloud.

2

Familiarity and Simplicity

Deep integration with Dynamics 365, Excel, Office 365, SharePoint, Power Automate, and PowerPoint shortens adoption time and improves user productivity.

3

Custom Visualizations

Developers can build and add custom visuals to shape dashboards and reports exactly around your business model and decision workflow.

4

Natural Language Interface

Power BI Q&A enables users to ask business questions in natural language and instantly receive visual answers without complex query skills.

5

APIs for Integrations

Power BI APIs let your team embed dashboards and reports inside enterprise web applications and digital products securely and at scale.

6

High Value

Start free with Power BI Desktop, then scale by licensing users or moving to Power BI Premium as data volume and reporting needs grow.
